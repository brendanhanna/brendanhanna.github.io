How It Works

Four Steps to Your Next Adventure

Simple, collaborative, and honestly kind of fun.

01

Tell Us Your Story

A relaxed 45-minute call where we learn about your family — who gets carsick, who needs a daily swim, who's dying to try rock climbing, and what "vacation" actually means to you.

02

We Design Your Adventure

Not a spreadsheet — a visual journey blueprint with mood boards, daily arcs, and the kind of details that make you start counting the days. Think of it as a story with your family as the main characters.

03

Refine Together

Swap the castle for a treehouse? Add a cooking class? Drop the museum? It's your trip. We refine until every piece feels right, then we handle all the bookings and logistics.

04

Go Live It

You're never on your own. Your dedicated Meridian guide is a text away — adjusting plans for weather, mood, or the serendipity of discovering the best gelato shop in the village.
